## Decisions taken without the human

- **Question:** What happens when all eligible mergers or reviewers are temporarily unavailable?
  **Selected:** recommended option `a`: queue the phase until an eligible investigator is free and no longer
  quota-paused; wait for the human only if no untried eligible member remains or the allowed replacement fails.
  **Why:** preserves the existing queue behavior and avoids human intervention for temporary unavailability.
  `codex-1`, `gemini-1` and `openrouter-haiku` recommended this in consultation `C1`; the user's subsequent
  autopilot instruction explicitly authorized taking the recommendation without another question.
