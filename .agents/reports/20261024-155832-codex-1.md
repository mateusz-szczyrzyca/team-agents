## Implementation

- `jobs.json` uses a versioned snapshot, atomic replacement and the same `bus.lock` as ordinary bus operations. Extracting `Bus.withLock` preserves existing bus transaction behavior without touching `state.json` during job operations.
- Missing job storage loads empty. Corrupt, unsupported or inconsistent storage returns the operation's typed `Storage` cause without overwriting it.
- Submission order, node declarations and submission/assignment high-water marks survive restart. Terminal jobs expire at age `30` days; active work remains.
- Snapshot timestamps use UTC RFC3339Nano, preserving instants without changing legacy `Timestamp` storage for ordinary bus data.
- Cancellation immediately stops queued/unacknowledged work. Running/waiting work retains its state and a durable `StopRequested` flag until the node reports a terminal result.
- Terminal reports clear that flag for `done`, `failed` and `stopped`, including completion winning the cancellation race. Repeated cancellation preserves history; cancellation never rewrites a completed outcome.
