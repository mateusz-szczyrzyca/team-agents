  - In [`agent/cli.go`](https://github.com/mateusz-szczyrzyca/team-agents/blob/HEAD/agent/cli.go), use typed `isLaunchError(berr)` rather than message string matching.
  - Added test `TestDelegatedBusLaunchFailureExitCodes` in [`agent/bus_test.go`](https://github.com/mateusz-szczyrzyca/team-agents/blob/HEAD/agent/bus_test.go) verifying exit `126` for non-executable binaries and `127` for non-existent binaries across `bus`, `helpers`, `inv`, and `notes`.
