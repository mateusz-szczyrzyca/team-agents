The human does not talk to you live. "Reply only with your questions" means here: write the questions to
`{{QUESTIONS_FILE}}` in the format described above (each starting with `## N. `, with a `**Recommended:**` part
and an empty `**Verdict:**` line) and end your turn with `STATUS: QUESTIONS`. Do not ask questions only in the text of your last message.
