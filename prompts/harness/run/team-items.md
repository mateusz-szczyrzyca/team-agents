## The items of your team

These are the items of the task list your team works on, each in full: what is to be done, the expected result and the
checks. The members take them one at a time; you do not ask a member for the text of its item, you have it here. What
a member hands you is finished when it does what its item says, the expected result is there and every check holds:
integrate and judge it against this text, not against what the code did before. When an item says that existing
behaviour or existing tests change, they change.
