# THE HUMAN ANSWERED THE QUESTIONS OF THE ROUND CHECKS

The harness applies the answers by itself. Where the human refused to allow something about a file, undo it on your branch, get the repository's gate green with `agent gate` and end your turn with `STATUS: DONE`. A commit that is already in the branch cannot be undone: where the human refused one, change nothing for it. Where they allowed everything, change nothing and end your turn with `STATUS: DONE`: the harness goes on with the round.
