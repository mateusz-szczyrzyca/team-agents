# YOUR UNCLEAR, SENT BACK ONCE

You ended with `STATUS: UNCLEAR`, but you looked at nothing: no file read, no command run. Your requester asks you
because it cannot spend its own tokens on it — questions it could answer by reading the files it named cost it more
than the work. Before you ask:

1. First read what the request names — its files, its paths, the commands it gives — and run what it asks to run.
2. Ask only what those leave open, and only what would change the result. What would not — the result's layout, a
   few lines more or less — choose yourself and say so in `Notes`.
3. Ask your requester first (`send_message`, then `wait_for_message` with its name, `timeout_seconds=120`).

Then do the request, or end with `STATUS: UNCLEAR` and the questions still open.
